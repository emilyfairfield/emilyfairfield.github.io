About Me

I'm a data scientist who delivers measurable results from complex, high‑stakes data. Over the past 7+ years with the U.S. Air Force and in healthcare, I've built machine‑learning and analytics solutions that improved F‑15 fleet availability by ~600 days per year and developed a multithreaded data translator that ran 10× faster, processed 6× more data, and reduced output size by 13×—enabling near real‑time aircraft health monitoring. My work has been recognized with honors including Junior Engineer of the Year (2022).

I hold an M.S. in Computer Science (Machine Learning) from Georgia Tech, a B.S. in Industrial & Systems Engineering from Georgia Tech, and an MBA from Kennesaw State. I'm hands‑on with Python, PyTorch, scikit‑learn, Dask, SQL, and the modern data stack. I care about reproducible experiments and communicating insights that drive decisions.
